# Sum up 23 November's world news in one image

Entry for make.fun bounty `news-image-20261123`.

The image is a 1024x1024 newspaper front page for Monday
23 November 2026, under the masthead THE WORLD BRIEF
(VOL. 01 | MONDAY | 23 NOVEMBER 2026), with text and
pictures:

1. COP31 ENDS WITHOUT CLIMATE PACT — two weeks of
   talks and two extra days end in deadlock over
   fossil fuel commitments. Source: United Nations.
2. NVIDIA REINSTATES JENSEN HUANG AS CEO — after
   initially rejecting his return, the board
   unanimously reinstates Huang following national
   security consultations. Source: NVIDIA.
3. INDIA PLEDGES $1.62 BILLION FOR NEPAL — New Delhi
   announces a $1.62 billion recovery package after
   floods caused an estimated $4.7 billion in damage.
   Source: Associated Press.
4. ASIAN MARKETS SLIDE AS SELL-OFF CONTINUES — market
   recap for 23 November 2026: KOSPI -3.3%,
   SENSEX -1.7% (late trading), SHANGHAI +0.1%,
   HANG SENG -0.1%, NIKKEI 225 CLOSED.
   Source: Market Data.

Each picture is labelled AI-GENERATED ILLUSTRATION.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no network, deterministic) writes
the image to `/output/entry.png`. The image bytes are
embedded in `main.py` as base64, so the entry is a single
self-contained program; the standalone image is also kept
as `image.png` in this working copy for reference.
